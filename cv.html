Experience

  1. 2021 – 2027

    Ph.D. Candidate, NIH F31 Fellow

    Guillemin Lab, Institute of Molecular Biology, University of Oregon

    Using genetic and pharmacological manipulation, live super-resolution imaging, scRNA-seq, and mitochondrial respirometry in zebrafish, ex vivo neonatal mouse islets, and cultured cells, I found that Beta-cell Expansion Factor A (BefA) permeabilizes β-cell membranes, drives calcium flux, and promotes mitochondrial function, resulting in β-cell proliferation and improved metabolic potential.

  2. 2019 – 2021

    Research Assistant

    Biology and the Built Environment Center, University of Oregon

    Led design, data collection, and analysis for a Thermo Fisher Scientific partnership that carried a prototype air sampler through five phases of Design-of-Experiments validation to commercial release as the AerosolSense sampler. Extended its validated range to influenza A, influenza B, and RSV, and ran a longitudinal human-subjects study of COVID-19-positive individuals through isolation. The university licensed the IP to Thermo Fisher with me as a named contributor.

  3. 2019 – 2020

    Research Technician

    Westerfield Lab, Institute of Neuroscience, University of Oregon

    Generated CRISPR/Cas9 knockout and knock-in zebrafish carrying patient alleles for the NIH Undiagnosed Diseases Network, modeling Usher syndrome and inherited sensory disease to test whether suspect variants cause disease.

  4. 2018 – 2019

    Undergraduate Research Assistant

    Biology and the Built Environment Center, University of Oregon

    Designed and led a hospital sampling campaign (several hundred swabs in under an hour), ran extraction, qPCR, and 16S library prep, and led the first-author paper showing room microbes track building layout and foot traffic, not window components.
